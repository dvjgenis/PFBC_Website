Our Mission
Our purpose is rooted in Scripture and driven by a desire to honor God in all that we do:
Worship & Ordinances: To worship God together as a unified body of believers and faithfully observe the ordinances ordained by Jesus Christ.
Proclaim the Gospel: To boldly share the Gospel of Christ—God’s power unto salvation for everyone who believes—both in season and out of season (Romans 1:16; 2 Timothy 4:2).
Discipleship & Education: To instruct all generations, young and old alike, on the whole counsel of God, guiding their faith and daily conduct (Acts 20:27).
The Great Commission: To actively carry out the Great Commission of our Lord, spreading His word within our local community and to the very ends of the earth (Matthew 28:19-20; Acts 1:8).
Community & Ministry: To establish, maintain, and operate ministries and institutions necessary to fulfill our calling and serve others.
